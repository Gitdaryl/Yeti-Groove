Straight answers for small business owners

What this isThe questions owners type into Google about getting found, answering customers and running the business, answered in plain words with the sources listed.

  • Getting foundWhy isn't my business showing up on Google Maps?Usually it is one of four things: the profile is not verified, its details are incomplete or wrong, Google sees other businesses as a better or closer match for the search, or the profile broke one of Google's rules. Google says local results are mainly based on relevance, distance and popularity, and there is no way to pay for a better spot.
  • Getting foundHow do I get my business to show up on ChatGPT?Make sure your website lets OpenAI's search crawler in, then make your business easy to describe: clear pages that say what you do and where, with the same name, phone number and details everywhere you are listed. For Google's AI answers, Google says there are no special tricks. If your page shows in normal search, it can appear there too.
  • Getting foundHow do I get more Google reviews without being pushy?Ask every customer, the day the job wraps, with a direct link or QR code to your Google review page, and reply to the reviews you get. Don't offer anything in return and don't ask only the happy customers: Google prohibits both.
  • Getting answeredHow do I get more leads for my contracting business?Before you pay for more leads, answer the ones you already get faster. A well-known Harvard Business Review study found that companies that tried to reach a lead within an hour were nearly seven times as likely to have a real conversation as those that waited even an hour longer. Then make sure your Google profile shows your work.
  • Getting answeredWhat happens when a customer calls my business after hours?Call your own number at seven tonight and find out. If it rings out or the voicemail is full, that customer is waiting on you while other businesses are a tap away. Make sure every after-hours call gets a quick reply: a text back within seconds, or an answering service or voice assistant that takes their details for the morning.
  • Getting it doneHow do I run my small business more efficiently?Follow one job from the first phone call to the final invoice, and write down every time someone types the same thing twice, chases something, or relies on memory. Fix the biggest of those first, usually by connecting the tools you already pay for, not by buying new ones.
