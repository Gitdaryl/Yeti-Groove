## 1. Scope of Work
Studio will produce the deliverables described in the proposal dated [DATE] (the "Proposal"), attached as Exhibit A. The Proposal's deliverables, scene counts, and timeline are the complete scope. Anything not listed is out of scope.

## 2. Fees and Payment
- Total fee: $[TOTAL], per the Proposal.
- Payment schedule: [50% deposit on signing; 50% on final delivery] / [40% on signing; 30% on rough-cut approval; 30% on final delivery].
- The deposit reserves the production slot and is non-refundable once pre-production begins.
- Invoices are due within [10] days. Final files and the live microsite transfer on receipt of final payment. Review drafts remain watermarked until then.

## 3. Revisions and Change Orders
- Two revision rounds are included. Additional rounds are $300 each.
- Changes to scope, scene count, or deliverables require a written change order quoted before work begins, including changes caused by revised architectural plans.

## 4. Client Responsibilities
- Deliver architectural plans, surveys, and brand assets by [DATE].
- Provide site access and permission for drone operations.
- Provide review feedback within [5] business days of each milestone. Delays extend the timeline day for day.
- Client warrants it has rights to all plans and materials supplied.

## 5. Visualizations
Portions of the deliverables are photoreal visualizations of Client's approved plans, not photographs of built conditions. Studio labels deliverables accordingly. Client is responsible for compliance of its own sales and marketing use, including any disclosure required for off-plan sales in its jurisdiction.

## 6. License and Ownership
- On full payment, Client receives a perpetual, worldwide license to use the final deliverables for marketing and sale of [PROJECT NAME], across all media.
- Studio retains ownership of project files, source materials, and working assets. Raw footage and source files are not deliverables.
- Studio may display the work in its portfolio and marketing after Client's public launch, unless agreed otherwise in writing.

## 7. Cancellation
If Client cancels after pre-production begins, the deposit is retained. If Client cancels after the shoot, 50% of the remaining balance is due in addition to the deposit.

## 8. Drone Operations
Aerial work is performed under FAA Part 107. Flights are subject to weather and airspace restrictions; affected shoot dates are rescheduled at no charge.

## 9. Limitation of Liability
Studio's total liability under this Agreement is limited to fees paid. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages.

## 10. General
This Agreement is governed by Michigan law. It is the entire agreement between the parties and may be amended only in writing signed by both.
