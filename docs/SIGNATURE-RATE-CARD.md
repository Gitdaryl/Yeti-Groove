Private. For proposals and quotes only. Public site shows tier starting prices, nothing else.
Last updated: 2026-08-02.

## Tiers (public starting prices)

### Signature Film - from $7,500
- Discovery + story treatment (one concept, approved before production)
- Half-day shoot: drone flyovers + ground reference of the site as it is
- Up to 8 photoreal visualization scenes from client plans
- 2 to 3 minute film, original composed score
- Cinematic presentation microsite on a yetigroove-managed URL
- Master file + one vertical social cut
- Two revision rounds
- Delivered in 2 to 3 weeks from plan handoff

### Development Package - from $15,000
Everything in Signature Film, plus:
- Up to 20 visualization scenes across phases
- 60 second cut for paid advertising
- Three vertical social cuts
- Still image pack: 10 print-resolution retouched renders (brochures, MLS, billboards)

### Sales Suite - from $25,000 (scope-priced)
Everything in Development Package, plus:
- Extended film + per-phase or per-unit spots
- Full image library for the sales office
- Custom domain with lead capture
- Quarterly update scenes as construction progresses (first year)

## Scope guards
- Visualization scene count is THE unit of work; state the cap in every quote
- Two revision rounds included, then $300/round
- Drafts are watermarked until final payment
- Renders always presented as visualizations of approved plans (off-plan protection)

## Floor check
- Internal floor: $50/hr effective. Track hours per commission; if a tier lands under floor, tighten the scene cap, do not discount the price.
- Never quote a commissioned film below $7,500.
