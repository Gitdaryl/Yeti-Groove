# Discovery Call Sheet - Signature Films

Use during the call, then report answers to Claude (any platform, Mobile is fine) immediately after while fresh. Claude scopes the quote from docs/SIGNATURE-RATE-CARD.md and drafts the proposal.

## The questions

**The project**
1. Project name, location, and parcel status today (raw land, cleared, under construction, phase X)?
2. What exists on paper: architectural renders, site plan, survey, floor plans? What format, and who supplies them?
3. What are you selling, and when (off-plan condos, lots, homes, memberships)? Sales launch date?

**The buyer and the use**
4. Who is the buyer, and where do they come from?
5. Where will the film show: sales office, website, paid ads, billboards, broker events? *(drives usage terms and cut list)*
6. Phases or units that need their own spots?

**Scope signals**
7. Timeline pressure? Hard dates (groundbreaking, sales event)? *(rush = +30%)*
8. Who approves creative, and how fast can they turn feedback? *(5 business day SLA)*
9. Budget conversation: present the tier that fits, then the one above it. Never quote below $7,500.

**Logistics**
10. Site access and drone permissions? Any airspace concerns?
11. Season/light: does the site show best now, or should the shoot window wait?

**Close**
12. Agree the next step on the call: "Proposal in your inbox by [day]."

## After the call, report to Claude
Say or type the answers in any order. Minimum needed to scope: parcel status, plans available, what they sell and when, where the film shows, phases, timeline pressure, approver. Claude returns the proposal draft from docs/PROPOSAL-TEMPLATE.md and logs the lead to Session Brain.
