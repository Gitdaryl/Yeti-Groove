About

Who We Are

Yeti Groove Media is a digital media and community engagement studio based in Devils Lake, Michigan. We design and operate web platforms that connect local businesses, event organizers, and residents — helping communities thrive online and in person.

Our flagship project is Manitou Beach, a community platform serving the Devils Lake / Irish Hills Lakes region of Michigan. The platform handles event listings, business directories, food truck scheduling, and neighborhood news for the area.

We also produce event coverage, branded digital media, and booking services for performing artists and live events.

SMS Policy

Text Message Communications

Yeti Groove Media uses SMS messaging to help local vendors and business operators manage their presence on our platforms. Text messages are sent only to individuals who have expressly provided their phone number and requested communication from us.

We do not send unsolicited text messages. All SMS communications are transactional or informational in nature — for example, sending a vendor their check-in link at their request.

Message types we send:

  • Check-in link delivery for food truck vendors (requested via our website)
  • Event-related reminders for opted-in organizers
  • Account or operational notifications for registered platform users

Opt-out: Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe from messages. Reply HELP for assistance. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

Carrier disclosure: Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Yeti Groove Media ("we", "us", "our") operates yetigroove.com and associated community platforms including manitoubeach.com. This policy describes how we handle personal information.

Information we collect:

  • Contact information (name, email, phone number) provided voluntarily through our platforms
  • Business information submitted for directory or scheduling purposes
  • Usage data collected via standard web analytics (page views, device type)

How we use your information:

  • To provide requested services (e.g., sending a check-in link via SMS)
  • To operate and improve our community platforms
  • To communicate operational updates to registered vendors and partners

We do not:

  • Sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes
  • Send unsolicited commercial text messages or emails
  • Store payment card data (all payments are processed by Stripe)

Data retention: We retain contact information only as long as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your data by emailing daryl@yetigroove.com.

Third-party services: Our platforms use Notion (data storage), Twilio (SMS delivery), Stripe (payments), and Vercel (hosting). Each operates under their own privacy policy.

Questions about this policy? Contact us at daryl@yetigroove.com.
