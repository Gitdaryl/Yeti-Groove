On fidelity, since you will ask

Generic image tools do not read a plan set. Ask one for a lake house and it returns a beautiful house that is not yours, with a roof pitch you never drew and windows you never specified. That is the whole problem, and it is the part of this work that takes the real hours.

Everything here is built against your drawings and held to them across all six scenes. Output is delivered and labelled as a visualization of approved plans, which protects you and your client both.

Plain terms

What this is not

Worth saying up front so nothing is a surprise later.

Not a site shoot

The pack is produced entirely from your drawings. No drone, no crew on the land, no footage of the site as it stands today.

Not a Signature Film

No original composed score, no presentation microsite, no print resolution still library. When a project needs those, it becomes a commissioned film and we quote it as one.

If the land itself deserves to be on camera, that is the Signature Film side of the studio.
