How This Works For You

You keep selling the way
you already sell.

Nothing changes about your print operation. You just add a line to the conversation you already have with your advertisers.

01

Bundle the free listing with every print ad

"Your ad also comes with a digital listing page and a QR code for the magazine." That's it. No extra pitch. It's included. Most will say yes because it costs them nothing.

02

Let the scan data do the upsell for you

Three months in, Billy's Bait Shop got 47 scans. You show him that number at renewal time. He asks what it would cost to get featured placement and SMS alerts. You tell him $25/mo. He says yes.

03

Stripe pays your cut automatically every month

No invoices. No manual transfers. Your share of every active paid listing lands in your bank account on the same day each month. It compounds as more advertisers upgrade.

04

Your 9 counties are locked — permanently

Nobody else can be a partner in your territory. Branch, Calhoun, Hillsdale, Irish Hills, Jackson, Lenawee, Steuben, LaGrange, St. Joseph — yours. As a founding partner, there's no annual fee. Ever.

05

Your revenue share grows as you grow

You start at 50%. Hit 20 active listings → 55%. Hit 40 → 60%. Hit 60 → 65%. The more you build the territory, the bigger your cut. At 40+ advertisers you're already at Gold tier.

06

Your territory is a real asset you own

You can grow it, sell it, or pass it on with Yeti Groove approval. It has a real dollar value — roughly 2x the annual digital income it generates. Build it for 3 years and see what it's worth.
