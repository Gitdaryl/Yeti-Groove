  • Your nine-county territory is locked free of charge. No annual territory fee until you have 30+ active listings — then it waives permanently.
  • If you ever want to expand into adjacent territories, you get first right of refusal at 50% off standard rates.
  • Your territory agreement is held by Lake Access Magazine as an entity — not you personally. It's transferable, inheritable, and can include partners like investors or co-publishers.
  • You set your own rate card. We have a platform floor ($9/mo minimum). You can charge more — and keep the margin above what you remit to us.
  • If your active listings ever drop below 10 for 90 consecutive days, we notify you first. You don't lose your territory without warning.
