Why isn't my business showing up on Google Maps?

Short answerUsually it is one of four things: the profile is not verified, its details are incomplete or wrong, Google sees other businesses as a better or closer match for the search, or the profile broke one of Google's rules. Google says local results are mainly based on relevance, distance and popularity, and there is no way to pay for a better spot.

YetiGroove Studio · Updated November 7, 2026

What Google looks at

Google is plain about this. It ranks local results on how well your profile matches the search, how far you are from the person searching, and how well known your business is.

“Local results are mainly based on relevance, distance, and popularity.”Google Business Profile Help

That third one includes reviews. Google says more reviews and positive ratings can help your local ranking, and so can other websites linking to you. You can't change how far a customer is from you, so the other two are where the work is.

Check these first

  • Is the profile verified? Google says verifying tells it you are authorized to represent the business, so it is more likely to show up in search results.
  • Are the details complete and correct? Google warns that if your info isn't accurate, your profile might not show up for relevant searches in your area. Check the hours, phone number, website and services.
  • Is your main category right? Google asks you to choose the fewest categories that describe your core business. A deck builder listed only as "Contractor" is easy to miss.
  • Is the name just your name? Adding extra words to the business name, like a town or a service, isn't allowed and can get the profile suspended.
  • Do you work at customers' homes? Then you are a service-area business. Google says to hide your address, and lets you set up to 20 service areas, no more than about two hours' drive from where you are based. It won't take a radius.
  • Is there more than one profile? Google allows one profile per business. Duplicates split your reviews and confuse it.

How long a fix takes to show

Don't expect to see changes the same afternoon. These are Google's own timings:

  • Verification reviews take up to 5 business days, and on rare occasions longer.
  • Edits usually take up to 10 minutes to review, but sometimes up to 30 days.
  • Service area changes may take up to 48 hours to show once approved.

If your profile has disappeared

If it was there and now it's gone, it may have been suspended or disabled. Google says it may suspend or disable profiles that don't follow its guidelines, and a removed profile can't be seen by the public.

“Appeals can only be submitted once.”Google Business Profile Help

So fix the cause before you appeal. The usual causes are the ones in the list above: extra words in the name, a home address shown for a service-area business, or a duplicate profile.
