Getting found

How do I get my business to show up on ChatGPT?

Short answerMake sure your website lets OpenAI's search crawler in, then make your business easy to describe: clear pages that say what you do and where, with the same name, phone number and details everywhere you are listed. For Google's AI answers, Google says there are no special tricks. If your page shows in normal search, it can appear there too.

YetiGroove Studio · Updated November 7, 2026

Let the right crawler in

ChatGPT's search uses a crawler called OAI-SearchBot to find websites. If your site blocks it, you won't be shown in ChatGPT search answers.

“To help ensure your site appears in search results, we recommend allowing OAI-SearchBot in your site's robots.txt file…”OpenAI

Your website person can check this in a minute. OpenAI says it can take about 24 hours for a change to that file to take effect.

There is a separate crawler, GPTBot, that collects pages for training AI models. Blocking GPTBot does not keep you out of search, so you can say no to training and still be found.

Google's AI answers work like normal search

Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode don't have a separate sign-up or a secret recipe.

“There are no additional requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, nor other special optimizations necessary.”Google Search Central

Google says a page has to be indexed and eligible to show in normal search first, and that showing up isn't guaranteed. So the work is the same work that gets you found on Google at all.

Be easy to describe

This part is our advice rather than a rule from either company. AI answers repeat what they can find and confirm, so give them something clear to find:

  • A page for each service you offer, in plain words, with the towns you cover.
  • The same business name, address, phone number and hours on your website, Google profile, Facebook page and any directories.
  • Recent reviews that mention the work you actually do.
  • Short, straight answers to the questions customers ask you, like this page.

Microsoft now shows site owners how often their pages appear in Copilot and Bing's AI summaries, in a free report inside Bing Webmaster Tools. It's worth setting up so you can see it change.
