How do I get more Google reviews without being pushy?

Short answerAsk every customer, the day the job wraps, with a direct link or QR code to your Google review page, and reply to the reviews you get. Don't offer anything in return and don't ask only the happy customers: Google prohibits both.

YetiGroove Studio · Updated November 7, 2026

Make it one tap

The biggest reason people don't leave a review is that it takes effort to find where. Google gives every business a direct link.

“To leave reviews, you can ask customers to visit a Google link or scan a QR code.”Google Business Profile Help

Text the link the same day you finish the job, while they're still pleased. Put the QR code on the invoice and on a card the crew can hand over. Customers need to be signed into a Google account to leave a review, so a phone usually works best.

Ask everyone, and ask the same way

Asking every customer is not pushy. Asking only the ones you think are happy breaks Google's rules. Google says businesses may not discourage negative reviews or selectively solicit positive ones.

“When soliciting reviews, merchants should not require or pressure users to leave ratings or write reviews while on the premises, nor should they request that specific content be included.”Google Maps

So no standing over someone while they type, and no telling them what to write. A short text with the link and a thank-you does the job.

What you can't do

“Offering incentives, like free or discounted goods or services, in exchange for customers to post reviews, change reviews, or remove negative reviews is considered fake & misleading content and is strictly prohibited.”Google Business Profile Help

You may read that the Federal Trade Commission allows some review incentives. It does, as long as the reward doesn't depend on what the review says. Google is stricter and bans incentives outright, so if you want reviews on Google, follow Google's rule.

Reviews from staff or family count as a conflict of interest under Google's policy, including former employees. Leave them out.

Reply to every review

Google says helpful replies show you're responsive, and the customer gets notified when you reply. Thank people by name for good ones. For a bad one, say what you will do about it, calmly, in two or three sentences. Other customers read the reply more closely than the review.

“A mix of positive and negative feedback often feels more trustworthy.”Google Business Profile Help
