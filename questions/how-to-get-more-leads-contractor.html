How do I get more leads for my contracting business?

Short answerBefore you pay for more leads, answer the ones you already get faster. A well-known Harvard Business Review study found that companies that tried to reach a lead within an hour were nearly seven times as likely to have a real conversation as those that waited even an hour longer. Then make sure your Google profile shows your work.

Speed decides most jobs

In 2011, researchers writing in Harvard Business Review sent a test inquiry to 2,241 U.S. companies and timed the replies. 37% answered within an hour. 24% took more than a day. 23% never answered at all. Among those that did reply within 30 days, the average wait was 42 hours.

A second study in the same article looked at 1.25 million leads. Companies that tried to make contact within an hour were nearly seven times as likely to reach a decision maker as those that tried an hour later, and more than 60 times as likely as those that waited a day or more.

The study is old and looked mostly at larger companies, not contractors. But anyone who has hired a contractor knows the pattern: the first one to call back usually gets the visit.

Show your work on Google

When someone searches for your trade, your Google profile is often the first thing they see, before your website.

“Photos help customers quickly understand the type of work you do. Add at least three photos that are representative of the services you offer.”Google Business Profile Help

Three is Google's minimum. Most good contractors have hundreds of job photos sitting on crew phones. Google also says the business must be verified for photos to show, and that complete, accurate profiles are more likely to appear in local results.

Make the first step easy

This is our advice from building these for trades. A customer ready to call should never have to hunt:

  • Your phone number at the top of every page, one tap to call on a phone.
  • A short form: name, phone, what they need. Every extra question loses people.
  • Every form and missed call answered by text within minutes, even if it's only to set a time to talk.
  • Reviews visible, because the next thing a customer does is check them.

Then think about buying leads

Paid ads and lead services send people to the same profile, the same reviews and the same phone. If those are slow or thin, you pay for leads that go to the competitor who answered first. Fix the leak, then turn up the tap.
